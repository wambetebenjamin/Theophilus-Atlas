# Image credits

Every photograph on Theophilus Atlas is a real, unwatermarked photograph published on
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) and used under the Pexels licence. No image on this
site was generated by artificial intelligence, and no watermark, signature shape or synthetic
overlay has been added.

Files live in `public/images/`. They were cropped and resized for the template: nothing else
was changed. Where the Pexels caption does not name the photographer, the credit reads
"on the Pexels page" and the linked source page carries the attribution.

People pictured are stock photography subjects. They are not staff, clients, patients or
beneficiaries of any organisation in the directory.

## Template design, fonts and icons

The layout, colour tokens, type scale, spacing, hover treatments and animation classes come
from the uploaded Atlas template and are used unchanged. Raleway and Oswald are served from
Google Fonts. Font Awesome 4.1 supplies the icon set and is bundled from the template package.

## Text and data

Build names, descriptions, clusters and delivery dates were compiled from the public
repositories of the one hundred builds. Cluster notes, the playbook, the stories and the
editorial copy on every page were written for this atlas.

The Pexels licence permits free commercial use without attribution. Attribution is given here
in full as a matter of practice. Anyone reusing one of these files should confirm the current
Pexels terms for that specific source page.
